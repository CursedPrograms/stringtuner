stringtuner

from the octobass to the violino piccolo

One tuner for every string instrument: C0 (16 Hz) to C8 (4186 Hz), within 0.4 cents.

Pick your instrument and it shows the open strings; play one and the nearest lights up. A needle for the cents, a strobe for the last bit of precision.

How it hears the pitch

  • YIN pitch detection, with the difference function done by FFT so the long window the lowest strings need (a fifth of a second) stays cheap.
  • Measured again exactly across as many periods as fit, so the highest notes, only a dozen samples long, still land within a fraction of a cent.
  • A big string with a weak fundamental doesn't fool it into reading an octave up.
  • Tested on every semitone from C0 to C8 at 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz: within 0.4 cents everywhere. pluginval strictness 10: passed.

Plug in

Standalone

  1. Unzip, run stringtuner.exe.
  2. Options > Audio/MIDI Settings: ASIO, your interface, the input your instrument is in.
  3. The input is live from the start; MUTE silences the output.

VST3

  1. Copy stringtuner.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  2. Rescan plugins in your DAW and put it on the instrument's track.

"Windows protected your PC"?

The files aren't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info > Run anyway.
